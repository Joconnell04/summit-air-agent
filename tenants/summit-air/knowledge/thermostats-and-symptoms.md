# Thermostats and common symptoms

## Thermostats

A thermostat tells the system when to run. A basic one has a dial or a few buttons. A programmable one changes the temperature on a schedule, and a smart one connects to Wi-Fi and an app. A blank screen often means dead batteries or a tripped breaker.

A thermostat in a poor spot, such as in direct sun, near a vent or on a drafty wall, reads the wrong temperature and can make the system run too long or too little. Steam and hot-water boilers usually have a thermostat on a wall and a control on the boiler itself that limits the water temperature.

Some smart thermostats need a common wire to work, and an older system may not have one.

## Furnace and air handler noises

- A loud bang from a furnace when it starts can be delayed ignition, which a technician should check.
- Squealing or grinding from a furnace or air handler is often a worn blower motor or belt.
- Rattling can come from a loose panel or a duct, and clicking at startup is normal as parts switch on.
- A hum or buzz from an outdoor air conditioner is common, but a loud buzz with no fan turning can be an electrical part that has failed.

## Smells

- A burning dust smell the first time the heat comes on in the fall is common and fades quickly.
- A musty smell when the air conditioner starts can come from moisture in the coil or drain pan.

## Heating symptoms

- No heat from a furnace can come from the thermostat, a tripped breaker, a dirty filter that shut the unit down, a pilot or ignition problem, or a failed part.
- Rooms that heat unevenly can come from closed vents, a clogged filter, or duct problems.
- Heat running but the home not warming up can come from a failing part or from drafty windows and poor insulation.
