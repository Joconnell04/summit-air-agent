# Summit Standard Membership

One plan. A caller is either a member or not.

- Two tune-ups a year: a heating tune-up in the fall or winter and a cooling tune-up in the spring or summer.
- Priority scheduling when something breaks.
- A discount on repairs, and no after-hours fee for members.

How to describe it in a line or two: "Our Summit Standard Membership covers two tune-ups a year and gets you priority scheduling if anything breaks. Want me to have someone tell you more?"

No price is quoted on the phone. Someone from the office calls back to sign people up; the agent never takes payment.
