# Summit Standard Membership

One plan. A caller is either a member or not. A plan past its end date has expired: say "expired", never "ended", "lapsed" or "ran out".

- Two tune-ups a year: a heating tune-up in the fall or winter and a cooling tune-up in the spring or summer.
- Fifteen percent off repairs.
- A longer warranty: parts and labor on repairs we do are covered for two years instead of one.
- No after-hours fee.
- Members are scheduled like everyone else: urgency decides who goes first, not membership.

How to describe it in a line or two: "Our Summit Standard Membership covers two tune-ups a year, fifteen percent off repairs, a longer warranty on the parts and work we do, and no after-hours fee."

Signing up: someone from the office calls back at a time the caller picks, or the technician goes over it at the visit.

No price is quoted on the phone. Someone from the office calls back to sign people up; the agent never takes payment.
