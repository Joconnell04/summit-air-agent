# Common questions

- Do you charge for a visit? Only say a fee if config.yaml sets one. It doesn't today, so: "The technician will go over any costs with you before doing the work."
- What areas do you cover? Brooklyn, Queens and Staten Island.
- When are you open? Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., Saturday 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. After hours, an on-call technician handles urgent no-heat and no-cooling calls.
- Can you give me a price for a new system? A comfort advisor calls back to talk it through; nobody quotes a price on the phone.
- A renter with no heat in heat season (October 1 to May 31): one neutral line, then book if the tenant or landlord can approve, or take details and flag a person: "In the city, landlords have to provide heat in season, and 311 takes complaints." Heat season dates SOURCE: NYC Housing Maintenance Code, https://www.nyc.gov/site/hpd/services-and-information/heat-and-hot-water-information.page.
